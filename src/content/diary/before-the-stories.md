I grew up crossing borders—literally.

I am the eldest child of an immigrant family, raised in western Türkiye between two citizenships, two cultures, and one crowded home. My grandparents were the rockstars of our family.

Before turning eighteen, I crossed international borders more than one hundred times. Moving repeatedly between places taught me that the same event can look completely different depending on where you stand.

Numbers became another language for me. I answered every mathematics question correctly in the national university entrance examination and went on to study mathematics.

The path was not linear.

I left and returned to university more than once, attempted several startups, completed my degree, and left a master's program during its second month.

Today, I work in finance and spend some of my free time supporting founders through startup acceleration.

Finance taught me structure. Startups taught me experimentation. Failure taught me to begin again.

Storytelling is where those lessons meet.
