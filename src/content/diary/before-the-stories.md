I grew up crossing borders, literally.

I am the eldest child of an immigrant family and raised in western Türkiye in a crowded home shaped by dual citizenship. 

My grandparents, the rockstars of our family, played a central role in raising me.

Before I turned eighteen, perhaps I crossed the exact same international border only more than eighteen times.

## My Linear Education

Moving repeatedly between cultures taught me perspective, perspective teached me numbers, numbers became language.

I answered every mathematics question correctly in the national bachelor entrance examination so chose to study mathematics. 

During my undergraduate years, I left and returned to university more than once, I attempted several startups, none became the success. Eventually, I completed my mathematics degree, then began a master's program on Logic, this time I knew what I do not want, I left during its second month.

## Where I Am Today

Today, daytimes I work in finance.

Nighttimes, I spend most of my free time with founders through startup acceleration.

These may appear to be separate worlds, It does not, clarity matters, information alone is rarely enough.

That realization led me more deeply into storytelling.

## Document of The Journey

My current goal is to compete in the Toastmasters national speech contest in Türkiye. Reaching that stage is not guaranteed. That uncertainty is part of why I want to document it.

I am not sharing this process as an expert who already knows the answers. I am sharing it as a student of storytelling.

Here, expect;

- lessons from studying storytelling
- story maps and writing exercises
- observations about audiences
- mistakes, rewrites, and mistakes, and rewrites.

And this, this is the beginning of my journey.
